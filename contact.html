Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about connecting with Bal Jyoti Foundation.

How can I contact Bal Jyoti Foundation?
You can reach out through our online contact form above, email us directly at info@baljyotifoundation.org, or call our team at +91 12345 67890.
Where is the Foundation located?
Bal Jyoti Foundation headquarters is situated in Bodhgaya, Gaya district, Bihar – 824231, India.
How can I volunteer?
You can submit a volunteer inquiry using our Contact Form with subject "Volunteer Opportunities", or visit our Join Us page to explore open volunteering programs.
How can I apply for an internship?
We offer student and research internships in craft development, community education, and digital storytelling. Send your CV and cover letter via the contact form or apply via our Join Us page.
How can I support the Foundation?
You can support us through direct bank transfer donations, purchasing artisan craft products, or sharing our community story with your network.
How can I collaborate with the Foundation?
We partner with ethical fashion brands, CSR foundations, and research institutions. Contact our partnerships team via the contact form with subject "Partnership & Collaboration".
