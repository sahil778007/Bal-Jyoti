QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about making a direct bank transfer donation.

How do I transfer money directly to the bank account?
You can use Net Banking, Mobile Banking app (NEFT / RTGS / IMPS), or visit your bank branch to transfer money to our Punjab National Bank account details listed above.
Will I receive an 80G Tax Exemption Receipt?
Yes! Bal Jyoti Foundation is a registered 12A & 80G non-profit organisation. Donations qualify for 50% tax deduction under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act. Please provide your PAN number when submitting receipt details.
How long does verification take after sending the receipt?
Our finance team verifies the credit within 24 business hours of receiving your transaction screenshot and emails your official tax receipt.
