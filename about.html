Our Journey & Story

OUR FOUNDATION STORY

Why We Exist

Bal Jyoti Foundation was established with a singular vision: to address the cycle of rural poverty by turning traditional crafts into premium sustainable enterprises.

In the remote villages of Bihar and Jharkhand, generations of artisans possess extraordinary craft capabilities. However, due to lack of market accessibility, unfair intermediary margins, and limited design resources, these traditions were dying, forcing young people to migrate to city slums for low-wage labor.

By establishing craft collectives, training centers, and design development programs, we build bridges between rural artisans and global patrons who value authentic, eco-friendly luxury handlooms.

What We Do

  • Providing free design training & craft looms.
  • Establishing fair-trade pricing directly to weavers.
  • Utilizing 100% natural, local organic cotton & bamboo fibers.
  • Funding primary education for all artisan children.
"Craft is not just decoration. It is dignity, heritage, and economic survival."

Our Mission

To empower marginalized rural communities by creating sustainable livelihoods, promoting gender equality, and upgrading traditional artisan skills to international design standards.

Our Vision

An inclusive, self-reliant rural India where cultural heritage is celebrated, and traditional craft clusters thrive as engines of modern green entrepreneurship.

Our Core Values

Artisan First

Ensuring maximum profit margins go directly to the hands that weave and shape our products.

Ecological Responsibility

Using 100% natural fibers, non-toxic dyes, and climate-neutral production cycles.

Cultural Heritage

Documenting, honoring, and preserving ancient Indian geometric and flatweave patterns.

Complete Transparency

Maintaining clear financial audits for all donor grants and retail income.

OUR MILESTONES

Decade of Progress

2016

The Humble Start

Opened our first handloom training shed in Bodhgaya with 5 spinning wheels and 3 weaver families below the poverty line.

2018

Bamboo Innovations

Introduced bamboo craft workshops to help farmers diversify income, creating structured baskets and luxury homeware.

2020

Women's Sewing Co-Op

Launched dedicated stitching and tailoring clusters. Provided over 150 women with sewing machines and commercial contracts.

2023

Global Showcase

Partnered with UNDP India to showcase our premium handloom rugs at international craft exhibitions, securing orders from European partners.

2026

Eco-Village Vision

Announced the establishment of 3 model Heritage Villages in Bihar to integrate craft production with eco-tourism and sustainable farming.

FOUNDER SPOTLIGHT

Rupak Kumar

Founder & CEO

"Our mission is to breathe new life into India's traditional crafts, transforming age-old artistry into sustainable livelihoods for rural communities."

I studied textile design at NIFT Hyderabad, and my passion has always been to revive India's traditional crafts while creating sustainable income opportunities for communities, especially those living below the poverty line (BPL).

Since starting the foundation in 2019, I've focused on empowering artisans by working with crafts like rugs, bamboo, and basketry. We aim to give them access to modern markets, improve their financial stability, preserve their cultural heritage, and help build leadership within their communities.

Mission Uplifting rural artisans through fair-trade market access & textile skill innovation.
Vision Self-reliant craft villages preserving heritage for generations to come.
