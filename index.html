Our Story & Core Objectives

Bal Jyoti Foundation was established to preserve India's rich craft heritage while providing sustainable, dignifying livelihood options. Many rural families face extreme economic hardships and lack stable opportunities. Our projects in Bihar and Jharkhand provide free, hands-on skills training, raw materials, and fair market linkages.

We work to ensure that artisans receive higher profit margins than typical wages. By involving rural communities directly in the business sector and promoting eco-friendly, natural materials (like organic cotton, bamboo, and local grasses), we secure stable livelihoods for families below the poverty line.

Your support allows us to expand our training centers, procure equipment, and establish design schools that make traditional art commercially viable in a modern world.
